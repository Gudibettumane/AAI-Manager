### Error Taxonomy:
1. **Conceptual mistake** (Fundamental physics/theory misunderstanding)
2. **Formula forgotten** (Could not recall formula)
3. **Formula misapplied** (Wrong formula for conditions of validity)
4. **Calculation error** (Arithmetic/algebra slip)
5. **Unit error** (Wrong multiplier, kV vs V, ms vs s, etc.)
6. **Sign convention error** (Passive sign convention, dot convention, leading/lagging)
7. **Reading mistake** (Overlooked "NOT", "EXCEPT", "TRUE/FALSE")
8. **Misinterpretation** (Misunderstood what the question asked)
9. **Guess** (Unsure, took an educated or blind guess)
10. **Time-pressure mistake** (Rushed due to time limit)
11. **Careless mistake** (Knew the answer but ticked the wrong option)
12. **Lack of knowledge** (Topic has not been studied yet)
