## 2. Competitive Exam Sourcing Strategy
Our questions are rigorously benchmarked against:
1. **GATE (EE/EC)** Previous Year Questions (1-mark conceptuals and CBT numericals)
2. **ESE Prelims (EE)** Objective Papers (Comprehensive coverage of power systems, machines, and measurements)
3. **PSU Recruitment Examinations:** Power Grid (PGCIL), NTPC, BHEL, ISRO, BARC, DRDO, DMRC
4. **CPWD & MES Engineering Services:** HVAC, Pumps, Substation, DG sets, Fire Fighting, and Contract Management
5. **AAI CBT Past Papers:** Junior Executive and Manager previous test questions
