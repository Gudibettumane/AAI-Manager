## 4. The 12-Point Error Taxonomy Reference

1. **Conceptual Misunderstanding:** Fundamental physics or electrical theory misconception (e.g. confusing star vs delta line/phase relations, direction of reactive power).
2. **Formula Recall Failure:** Complete inability to retrieve the equation from memory under exam conditions.
3. **Formula Misapplication:** Recalling a valid formula but using it outside its valid domain (e.g. using Maximum Power Transfer for variable source resistance).
4. **Calculation / Arithmetic Slip:** Algebraic manipulation or numerical arithmetic error during execution.
5. **Unit / Multiplier Error:** Inadvertently mixing units ($kV$ instead of $V$, $\mu F$ instead of $mF$, or failing to convert minutes to seconds).
6. **Sign Convention Error:** Overlooking passive sign convention in circuit equations, dot convention aiding/opposing, or leading/lagging power factor signs.
7. **Reading Mistake:** Missing qualifying trigger words such as "NOT", "EXCEPT", "INCORRECT", or "MINIMUM".
8. **Misinterpretation:** Misunderstanding what quantity was actually requested by the examiner.
9. **Guessing:** Submitting an answer without mathematical proof or definitive conceptual certainty.
10. **Time-Management Failure:** Rushing calculations or running out of time on a 60–90 second CBT question.
11. **Memory Interference:** Confusing two related equations or conventions (e.g. $Z$-parameters vs $Y$-parameters, or series vs parallel resonance $Q$-factor).
12. **Lack of Knowledge:** Topic or standard has not yet been covered in study blocks.
